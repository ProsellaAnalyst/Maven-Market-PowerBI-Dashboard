# Maven Market | Revenue, Risk & Smarter Decisions

**What happens when some products are quietly draining your profit while others are carrying the whole business?**

Built using **Power BI** and data from **Maven Analytics**, this 3-dashboard suite goes beyond surface-level performance to uncover which products are worth keeping, which need fixing, and what actually happens when you change the numbers.

---

## 1. Project Overview & Dashboard

This project analyzes Maven Market's product performance across brands, geographies, and KPIs — with a focus on identifying high return rate products, low profit margins, and simulating smarter pricing and cost decisions.

**Key Metrics at a Glance:**
- Total Revenue: **$1.8M** vs Target: **$1.7M**
- Total Profit: **$661,159**
- Total Transactions: **167,616**
- Average Profit Margin: **59.93%**
- Total Returns: **7K** | Return Rate: **0.99%**
- Current Month Profit: **$71,682** | Transactions: **18,325**

---

## 2. Problem Area

Maven Market had products performing at very different levels — some with strong profit margins and low return rates, others with high returns and profit that barely justified their cost.

**Core issues uncovered:**
- High return rates on specific brands eating into overall profit
- Low profit margins on certain products relative to their cost
- Over-reliance on top-performing brands without visibility into underperformers
- No clear mechanism to test the financial impact of pricing or cost changes before making decisions

---

## 3. Who Is at Stake & What's at Risk

**Primary Stakeholders:** Executive leadership, Sales & Category managers, Finance teams

**What's at risk:**
- Continued investment in low-profit, high-return products
- Revenue targets masked by top performers hiding weaker brands
- Missed opportunity to grow profit through smarter pricing and cost reduction
- Inability to predict outcomes before making operational changes

---

## 4. Why Return Rate & Low Profit Margin Is a Problem

A high return rate is not just a logistics issue — it directly reduces net revenue and signals product-market fit problems. When paired with a low profit margin, it means Maven Market is spending more to sell a product than the profit it generates.

The issue is not just underperformance — it is **undetected underperformance** sitting alongside strong brands, making the overall numbers look healthier than they are.

---

## 5. Big Idea + Approaches

**Big Idea**
Filter out the noise. Identify what is genuinely driving profit, what is dragging it down, and simulate what smarter decisions would actually look like in numbers.

**Decision Intelligence Approaches:**
- Brand-level profit and return rate breakdown to surface underperformers
- What-If scenario simulation across four levers: transactions, returns, cost per unit, and pricing
- Geographic KPI tracking to understand regional performance patterns
- Weekly revenue trend analysis to catch volatility hidden in monthly totals

---

## 6. Key Findings & Recommendations

**Findings from the Dashboards:**

- **Hermanos** generated the highest revenue across all brands
- **Horatios** leads in profit efficiency — 58% profit margin with only 1.25% return rate
- **James Bay** had low profit but the lowest return rate — a candidate for cost optimization not discontinuation
- **King's Brand** had the highest return rate and lowest profit — highest risk product
- All Top 30 products met or exceeded their targets
- USA dominates revenue at **$181K**, followed by Mexico (**$73K**) and Canada (**$16K**)
- Simulated profit impact: reducing cost per unit by 50% while keeping returns constant → profit rises significantly

**Recommendations:**

1. **Prioritize Horatios-style products** — high margin, low returns. These are the benchmark
2. **Investigate King's Brand** — high return rate + low profit = immediate review needed
3. **Optimize James Bay's cost structure** — low returns suggest customer satisfaction; the issue is cost, not product quality
4. **Use the What-If simulator before any pricing change** — test impact on profit before committing
5. **Invest in USA market expansion** — already the strongest region; scaling here has the highest ROI
